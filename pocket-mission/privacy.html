Privacy Policy

Small acts. Your choices. Your privacy.

Updated November 23, 2026

The short version

Pocket Mission helps you complete small daily acts of kindness. You can use it without creating an account. Your challenges, streaks, rest days, and reminder preferences are stored on your device. The app includes optional tips, which are processed through Apple.

Information Pocket Mission stores on your device

Pocket Mission stores selected challenges, completed deeds, streak and protected-day records, rest-day choices, preferences, and the status of its welcome screen using local app storage. Pocket Mission does not ask you to upload photographs or submit proof of your good deeds. Removing the app can remove this locally stored information, subject to device backups.

Advertising and tracking

Pocket Mission does not display advertisements or use third-party advertising SDKs. It does not request tracking permission for advertising. The app does not require an account or collect proof of good deeds.

Apple purchases

Optional developer tips are processed by Apple through in-app purchases. BAD Hollow Studios receives purchase-related information made available by Apple to administer transactions but does not collect your payment card number directly in Pocket Mission. Tips do not provide additional challenge access or change streak results.

Support messages

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide. We use that information to respond and handle your request. Avoid sharing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information in support messages.

Sharing, retention, and your choices

We do not require an account or a personal profile to use Pocket Mission. Apple handles optional in-app purchases under its applicable policies. Local app records remain on your device until removed or altered, subject to device backups; support correspondence may be retained as reasonably needed to resolve inquiries or meet legal obligations. You can contact us about information we control and applicable privacy rights.

Children and international data processing

Pocket Mission is a general-audience app and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact us. Optional Apple in-app purchases may be subject to age-related restrictions. Service providers may process information in other countries under their own privacy practices and applicable law.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as Pocket Mission’s features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when we do. Privacy inquiries: admin@badhollowstudios.com.
