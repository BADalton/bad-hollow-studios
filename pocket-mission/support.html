Need a hand?

Having trouble with daily challenges, goal settings, reminders, rest days, the streak calendar, advertising, or optional tips? We’re happy to help.

Email us at admin@badhollowstudios.com. Put “Pocket Mission” in the subject so we can find your message quickly.

Helpful details to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The Pocket Mission version shown in Settings
  • What you tapped, what happened, and what you expected
  • A screenshot of the problem if it helps (no private information needed)

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved? Your challenge history, streaks, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app may erase that history.

Why isn’t a rewarded ad available? Streak protection is optional and only appears for eligible missed days. An ad must be available and the reward must be confirmed before the day is protected. Your ordinary challenges still work without ads.

Why can’t I see tip amounts? Optional tips depend on Apple’s in-app purchase availability. No tip is required to use Pocket Mission.

How do I change reminders? Open Pocket Mission → Settings to change notification times, quantity, and scheduled rest days. You can also manage notification permissions in iPhone Settings.
