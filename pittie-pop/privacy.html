Privacy Policy

Pittie Pop · A little joy, one pop at a time.

Effective date: October 13, 2026

BAD Hollow Studios operates Pittie Pop, a mobile puzzle game. We respect your privacy and want you to understand how information may be used while you play.

Information and game progress

Pittie Pop does not require a player account to enjoy the game. Game progress and preferences may be stored on your device. If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and a description of the issue.

Advertising and third-party services

Pittie Pop uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers where permitted, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information. This can be used for delivering and measuring ads, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, ad personalization.

Learn more about Google's practices in its Privacy Policy and information about partner apps and sites.

Consent and privacy choices

Depending on your location and device, privacy and advertising consent choices may be presented when you use Pittie Pop. Consent is voluntary where consent is requested. On iOS, you can also manage app tracking permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For requests to withdraw advertising consent, exercise applicable privacy rights, or ask about your choices, contact privacy@badhollowstudios.com. Additional in-app privacy controls may be provided as supported by the advertising consent system.

How information is shared

Information processed for advertising may be shared with Google and participating advertising providers. Support communications are used to respond to requests. We do not require you to submit personal contact information to play.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game data may remain until reset or removal of the app, subject to device backups. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to resolve requests and maintain business records. Third-party advertising providers maintain their own retention policies.

You may contact us to request access to or deletion of information we control, subject to applicable legal obligations. You may also have additional privacy rights depending on your location.

Children and families

If you are a parent or guardian with questions about your child's privacy while using Pittie Pop, please contact us. Advertising and consent practices may vary according to applicable age-related requirements.

International processing

Advertising providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their privacy practices and applicable legal requirements.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when Pittie Pop's features or data practices change. The effective date above will reflect the latest version.
