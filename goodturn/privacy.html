Privacy Policy

Small acts. Your choices. Your privacy.

Effective October 14, 2026

The short version

GoodTurn helps you complete small daily acts of kindness. You can use it without creating an account. Your challenges, streaks, rest days, and reminder preferences are stored on your device. The app also offers ads and optional tips, which use third-party services.

Information GoodTurn stores on your device

GoodTurn stores selected challenges, completed deeds, streak and protected-day records, rest-day choices, preferences, and the status of its welcome screen using local app storage. GoodTurn does not ask you to upload photographs or submit proof of your good deeds. Removing the app can remove this locally stored information, subject to device backups.

Advertising and reward videos

GoodTurn uses Google AdMob for banner ads and optional rewarded ads that can protect an eligible missed streak day. Google and its advertising partners may process data such as IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers when permitted, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and approximate location inferred from network information. They may use information for advertising delivery, measurement, personalization where permitted, security, and fraud prevention. Their practices are described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s partner information.

Consent and advertising choices

Where required, GoodTurn requests advertising consent through Google’s consent system before loading ads. When available or required, you can revisit your advertising privacy choices in the app’s Settings. iOS also provides app tracking controls under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads may not always be available, and declining optional consent does not prevent you from doing daily kindness challenges.

Apple purchases

Optional developer tips are processed by Apple through in-app purchases. BAD Hollow Studios receives purchase-related information made available by Apple to administer transactions but does not collect your payment card number directly in GoodTurn. Tips do not provide additional challenge access or change streak results.

Support messages

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide. We use that information to respond and handle your request. Avoid sharing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information in support messages.

Sharing, retention, and your choices

We do not require an account or a personal profile to use GoodTurn. Third-party advertising providers and Apple handle information under their own applicable policies. Local app records remain on your device until removed or altered, subject to device backups; support correspondence may be retained as reasonably needed to resolve inquiries or meet legal obligations. You can contact us about information we control and applicable privacy rights.

Children and international data processing

GoodTurn is a general-audience app and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact us. Advertising and purchase features may be subject to age-related restrictions. Service providers may process information in other countries under their own privacy practices and applicable law.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as GoodTurn’s features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when we do. Privacy inquiries: admin@badhollowstudios.com.
